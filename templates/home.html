FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is this a medical diagnosis?

No. The system gives a risk estimate for awareness only. It cannot replace a doctor's diagnosis, so always consult a qualified healthcare professional.

How accurate are the predictions?

Each model is trained on public medical datasets. Accuracy depends on the model and on the quality of the values you enter, so treat results as guidance, not certainty.

What information do I need?

Health values such as those found in a recent blood test or check-up report. Each module lists the inputs it needs.

Is my data safe?

Your account is protected with secure login, and your prediction history is visible only to you.

Do I need to create an account?

Yes. An account lets you save predictions and view your history later.

Medical disclaimer: MULTI provides machine-learning-based risk estimates for educational purposes only. Results are not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
