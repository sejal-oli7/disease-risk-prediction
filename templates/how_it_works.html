HOW IT WORKS

A Simple Flow from Health Data to Visualization

Follow a simple step-by-step process to understand how MULTI processes health information and provides risk predictions.

01

Create an Account

Register securely and create your MULTI account to access the prediction features.

02

Enter Health Information

Provide the required health parameters such as age, blood pressure, glucose level, BMI, and other relevant data.

03

Select a Disease

Choose the disease prediction module you want to test.

04

Run Prediction

The backend processes the submitted health information using the selected machine learning model.

05

View Result

View the predicted risk level, probability, and visual feedback about the prediction.

06

Track History

Previous prediction results remain available in your account so you can review your prediction history.
